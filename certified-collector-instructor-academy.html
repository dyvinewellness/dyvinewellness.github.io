Common Questions

Collector & Instructor Academy FAQs

Do I need a medical background or prior experience?
No. Every procedure is taught step by step with live demonstrations, hands-on practice, and competency checks. Motivated beginners and experienced healthcare professionals train side by side.
What certification do I receive?
You receive a certificate of completion documenting your training across all five skill tracks, and you must successfully complete the required mock demonstrations to earn it. A certificate of completion is not a government license or accreditation — in class we cover how registration, employer, and industry requirements work so you know exactly what applies to your goals.
Can I really start a business after one weekend?
You'll leave with trained skills, a written action plan, and lifetime access to the business tools in the VIP portal — startup checklists, forms, vendor lists, pricing worksheets, and marketing templates. How far and how fast you take it depends on your effort and your market; mentorship opportunities are available for graduates who want continued guidance.
What should I bring, and what's the dress code?
Bring a notebook and pen, and wear comfortable professional attire. Hands-on participation is required both days, and photos or videos may be taken during training for educational and promotional purposes.
Is the training in person? Where?
Yes — training is live and in person at Dyvine Medical & Wellness, 9070 Peridot Parkway, Stockbridge, GA 30281, convenient to the greater Atlanta metro area.
