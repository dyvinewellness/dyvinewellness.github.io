Getting Started

General Questions

Do I need a medical background to enroll?
No. Our flagship programs are designed for motivated beginners as well as experienced professionals. Every skill is taught step by step with live demonstrations, hands-on practice, and competency checks.
What do I receive when I complete a program?
A certificate of completion documenting your training, comprehensive course manuals, and lifetime access to the VIP student portal — business templates, forms, checklists, scripts, vendor lists, and marketing tools. A certificate of completion documents your training; it is not a professional license or government accreditation.
Will this training license me or guarantee a job or income?
No training program can promise that, and we won't either. DMW Academy provides professional training, business education, and real tools. Employment, contracts, clients, and income depend on your effort, your market, and any licensing or registration requirements in your state and industry — which we help you understand in class.
Where are classes held? Is training in person?
Yes — training is live and in person at Dyvine Medical & Wellness, 9070 Peridot Parkway, Stockbridge, GA 30281, convenient to the greater Atlanta metro area. Corporate training can also be delivered on-site at your location.
What should I bring to class?
A notebook and pen, and comfortable professional attire. Hands-on participation is required. GLP-1 Accelerator students may also want a laptop or tablet for the workshops. Photos and videos may be taken during training for educational and promotional purposes.
How do I access the student portal?
Enrolled students receive portal access instructions by email. Already enrolled? Use the Student Portal link in the site menu to log in.
Program-Specific

GLP-1 Business Accelerator

Will I be able to prescribe or sell GLP-1 medications after this course?
No. Only licensed healthcare providers can prescribe or dispense medication, and this program does not change that. You'll learn how compliant GLP-1 wellness businesses actually work — provider partnerships, telehealth, and pharmacy relationships handle the medical side while you build the coaching, accountability, and client-experience side.
I already run a wellness or beauty business. Is this still for me?
Yes — many students are existing business owners adding a GLP-1 wellness program as a new service line. The modules on program design, CRM, marketing, and scaling apply directly to an established business.
What's actually covered in the two days?
Thirteen modules: the GLP-1 industry, understanding the medications, compliance and scope of practice, program building, provider partnerships, client onboarding, coaching and accountability systems, client portals, CRM and automations, sales consultations, marketing, content creation with AI tools, and scaling. Every module includes a hands-on workshop.
Program-Specific

Certified Collector & Instructor Academy

What certification do I receive?
A certificate of completion documenting your training across all five skill tracks. You must successfully complete the required mock demonstrations to earn it. In class we cover how registration, employer, and industry requirements work so you know exactly what applies to your goals.
What are the five skill tracks?
Drug testing specimen collection (DOT, Non-DOT & Rapid), CPR instructor training, ink fingerprinting (FD-258), DNA specimen collection, and business startup & mobile setup — including LLC, EIN, vendors, lab accounts, and marketing.
What are the 5 mock scenarios?
Observed collection, donor refusal, temperature out of range, suspected tampering, and rapid testing errors — practiced live under instructor guidance before you ever face them with a real client.
Can I really start a business after one weekend?
You'll leave with trained skills, a written action plan, and lifetime access to the business tools in the VIP portal. How far and how fast you take it depends on your effort and your market; mentorship opportunities are available for graduates who want continued guidance.
