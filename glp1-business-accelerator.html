Why This Program

The Wellness Industry Is Moving Fast. Get Positioned — the Right Way.

GLP-1 programs are reshaping the weight-management industry — and clients need trustworthy, well-run wellness businesses to support them. The problem? Most people who feel called to this work don't know how to build it compliantly.

Common Questions

GLP-1 Accelerator FAQs

Do I need a medical license or medical background?
No medical background is necessary. The program is designed for entrepreneurs, coaches, and healthcare professionals alike. If you are licensed, you'll learn how your credentials fit into the model; if you're not, you'll learn how to build a compliant business that works alongside licensed providers.
Will I be able to prescribe or sell GLP-1 medications after this course?
No. Only licensed healthcare providers can prescribe or dispense medication, and this program does not change that. What you'll learn is how compliant GLP-1 wellness businesses actually work — including how provider partnerships, telehealth, and pharmacy relationships handle the medical side while you build the coaching, accountability, and client-experience side.
What exactly do I get when I finish?
A certificate of completion, your course materials and templates, and lifetime access to the student portal. Your certificate documents the training you completed; it is not a license or accreditation, and no program can guarantee income or clients.
I already run a wellness or beauty business. Is this still for me?
Yes — many students are existing business owners adding a GLP-1 wellness program as a new service line. The modules on program design, CRM, marketing, and scaling apply directly to an established business.
What should I bring?
A notebook, a pen, and a laptop or tablet if you'd like to work digitally during the workshops. Come ready to participate — every module includes hands-on activities, and you'll leave with real drafts of your program, marketing plan, and growth plan.
