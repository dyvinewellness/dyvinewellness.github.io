Programs Built Around Your People

Choose a ready-made program or work with us to design training that fits your industry, compliance needs, and schedule.

❤

CPR & First Aid Certification Days

Group certification in CPR AED, First Aid, Pediatric CPR, and Bloodborne Pathogens — ideal for workplaces, schools, daycares, gyms, and community programs. We bring the equipment and run the entire day.

🧪

Drug Testing Program Readiness

Train your staff in professional specimen collection procedures — DOT and Non-DOT protocols, chain-of-custody documentation, problem collections, and rapid testing — to support your internal testing program.

🧬

DNA Collection & Fingerprinting Training

Equip your team with buccal swab collection technique, chain-of-custody handling, and FD-258 ink fingerprinting skills — with hands-on practice and competency checks.

📈

Workforce Development Partnerships

We partner with workforce boards, nonprofits, re-entry programs, and community organizations to deliver career-skills training that helps participants move into healthcare-adjacent service work.
